02 / The brand idea

A thoughtful way
to move forward.

02

Mighty connects professional intention with people, evidence, and a considered next action.

A clear goal.
A person worth understanding.
A conversation with a reason.
A next step remembered.

Intentional

Start with what the person wants to accomplish. Career moves, fundraising, and building a team can coexist as separate goals. Keep the relevant goal visible when explaining a recommendation.

Intelligent

Make the supporting evidence legible. Distinguish observed facts, user knowledge, unanswered questions, and model suggestions. Specific reasons earn trust; invented certainty spends it.

Human

Help someone prepare and follow through. Leave room to edit, decide, save, copy, and record what happened. The product should respect the person on each side of the conversation.

For professional goals

Mighty serves people pursuing consequential professional goals. A student, alumni, or cohort network can be an initial route to customers. It does not define an MBA-only product or the limits of the market.

A useful association

The circles can suggest two distinct people with something in common. That is an interpretation of the chosen shape, not a claim about its original designer’s intent. Let consistent use and considerate product behavior establish meaning.

Positioning line: Mighty. Intentional, intelligent networking. Use it in introductions and explanatory material. Routine screens should lead with the work the person came to do.

The confirmed wordmark

Native setting: 19 px, weight 800, tracking −0.3 px, with a 10 px symbol gap. A double-size lockup scales those values together to 38 px, −0.6 px, and 20 px. The supplied outlined wordmark uses the actual bundled font, not a substitute or a re-created letterform.

A working reading system

The display examples above illustrate this guide. They are not newly verified product typography rules. For routine interfaces, start with 36/42 page headings, 24/30 section headings, 16/24 body, 14/20 controls, and 12/18 secondary metadata. Adapt to viewport and real content.

Reading rhythm

Use sentence case, left alignment, normal tracking in prose, and roughly 65 characters per line for sustained reading. Keep critical warnings at a readable size. Use weight and space to establish hierarchy before adding another color or another typeface.

Portable, licensed files

The guide embeds the existing unmodified Schibsted Grotesk Latin variable WOFF2. Its bundled range is 400 to 900, and the wordmark uses 800. Include the SIL Open Font License when sharing the font. Outlined SVGs need no font installation. Browser shaping can differ slightly from the exported outlines.

The publisher describes a digital-first family with editorial roots. Schibsted Grotesk project · Included font license.

07 / Voice

Clear about the reason.
Careful with the promise.

07

Write like a capable colleague helping someone make a considered professional move. Lead with a useful action and keep its evidence close.

Grounded reason / illustrative
“Their current role is relevant to your goal. We have no source for the team’s hiring plans yet.”

A contact’s role can support a reason to talk. It does not establish an available job.

Human control / illustrative
“Your draft is ready to edit. Copy it when you’re happy with the message.”

Preparation, copying, sending, and recording a sent message are separate actions.

Specific

Name the relevant goal, what was observed, and what is missing. A short factual reason is more useful than a polished claim of perfect fit. Do not describe a person as a score or imply a calibrated probability.

Considerate

Talk about people and conversations. Keep the user’s judgment visible. Use “Things you’ve learned” for personal context, and “No source for this yet” for missing evidence. Avoid language that treats someone as inventory.

Honest

Distinguish a device save from a confirmed account save, and an estimate from a measured result. Say what actually happened. Use Assist consistently when naming an allowance and show applicable usage before a metered action.

Use US English and direct verbs. Avoid inflated promises, forced familiarity, and first-person narration by Mighty. Separate the user’s intended outcome from a fact about the other person.

Keep the source close.

Make factual content, interpretation, and unanswered questions visually distinct. Color alone should never carry that distinction.

Explain the relationship.

Use simple connectors, direct labels, and a legend when a line style has meaning. A dashed outline can mark a proposed next step.

Layout and surfaces

Use paper for the page, white for focused content, and indigo for the primary point of action. A working spacing sequence is 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, and 64 px. Treat proposed 8 px control, 12 px card, and 16 px dialog corners as a starting system, not a newly approved app refactor.

Images and diagrams

Prefer real, authorized product context and explanatory diagrams. Keep imported personal details out of public examples. Avoid decorative networks, AI sparkles, glowing brains, and relationship activity that has not happened. Every visual should make a useful point.

Numbers and evidence

Label units, dates, populations, and assumptions beside a chart. Separate observed results from planning scenarios using words and line styles. A decorative accent does not convert an estimate into a measured outcome.

Motion with a purpose

Use brief, restrained feedback when it explains an action. A working duration is 120 to 180 ms. Keep the logo still and respect reduced-motion preferences. Show progress only when it reflects actual work.

10 / Care & access

Protect the form.
Respect the person.

10

Consistency comes from a small set of stable decisions. Usability comes from checking how those decisions work in the real interface and the final medium.

Do not stretch. Keep the 31:22 aspect ratio.

Do not rotate. Preserve the original horizontal relationship.

Do not separate. The shared area belongs to the construction.

Do not outline. Use the supplied solid masters.

Mighty

Do not substitute type. Keep the approved Schibsted lettering.

Do not fade the mark. Preserve opaque fills and the correct reproduction mode.

Keyboard and accessible names

A logo link should have a clear name such as “Mighty home.” Avoid duplicate announcements from a decorative symbol beside the visible name. Keep keyboard focus obvious, label controls, and describe completion or errors with words.

Readable controls

Use 44 px as a comfortable working touch-target size. Check compact controls against WCAG 2.2’s 24 CSS px minimum or its stated spacing exceptions. Reflow ordinary content at narrow widths and test text zoom, keyboard navigation, and extension panels.

Screen and print

Check transparent edges, tiny-size legibility, neighboring colors, and full-name clipping. For print, use a suitable vector and the printer’s profile. Approve an actual-size physical proof; this digital guide does not establish universal print conversions.

Release care

Keep source files versioned and names unambiguous. Review any logo, favicon, or extension icon replacement as an explicit asset change. Use authorized examples and preserve the distinction between a proposed rule and a checked implementation.

W3C target-size guidance. The checks above are practical review requirements. This book does not claim complete screen-reader, browser, print, or accessibility certification.

What is confirmed, and what remains guidance?

The selected logo and native wordmark settings are recorded from the Mighty source on September 12, 2026. The current kit records its exact SVG and font hashes, dimensions, rendered pixel samples, transparent corners, and distinct weight-800 outlines. This edition updates the presentation and explanation on September 13, 2026. It does not redraw the identity.

Clear space, working type scales, interface corners, comfortable target sizes, and layout rhythm are guidance. No new audience-recognition study, minimum-size certification, live-browser pixel parity, print proof, or complete accessibility certification is claimed.

The source uses multiply. The portable master freezes the overlap color on white. The current 40 × 40 icon canvas holds a 31 × 22 mark, or 77.5% of its width. The historical favicon has different proportions. These distinctions remain part of the asset record.
